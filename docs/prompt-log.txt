6 OCTOBER 2026 - OPTIONAL RATE LIMITING
============================================================

Tool:
ChatGPT

Prompt / Question:
Asked for technical support selecting a low-risk optional extension after the
mandatory functionality was complete and how basic flood protection could be
added without interfering with the required protocol and file-transfer logic.

Support received:
Per-client rate limiting was discussed for the chat-message commands BCAST,
PMSG, and RMSG.

The selected behaviour was:

- Allow up to 10 chat-message requests per client within a five-second window.
- Return ERR 023 RATE_LIMITED NID:2557 when the limit is exceeded.
- Keep REGISTER, LIST, JOIN, LEAVE, ROOMS, SENDFILE, and QUIT outside the
  chat-message rate limit.
- Maintain rate-limit state independently for each connected client.

How I used the response:
I developed and reviewed the change on a separate Git branch named
optional-rate-limit rather than modifying the main branch immediately.

I tested a burst of 11 broadcast messages. The first 10 were accepted and the
11th returned:

ERR 023 RATE_LIMITED NID:2557

After waiting for the five-second rate-limit window to reset, another
broadcast message was accepted successfully.
